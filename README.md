# Strategic Analysis of the U.S. Electric Vehicle Market 🚗⚡

**Author:** Preetinder Singh

## 📊 Overview
This Power BI project provides a comprehensive, interactive analysis of the United States Electric Vehicle (EV) market. By analyzing over 150,000 vehicle records, this dashboard explores historical adoption trends, manufacturer dominance, geographic hotspots, and the distribution of battery capabilities.

## 📈 Dashboard Features & Visualizations

### Page 1: High-Level Market Overview
* **Market KPIs:** Top-level metrics tracking total vehicle volume, average electric range, and the split between Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) and Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs).
* **Historical Growth:** A dynamic line chart illustrating the year-over-year growth of EV adoption by model year.
* **CAFV Eligibility:** A donut chart breaking down the fleet by Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle eligibility.
* **Geographic Distribution:** A filled map highlighting the concentration of EVs across different states.
* **Manufacturer Leaderboard:** A horizontal bar chart ranking the top 10 EV manufacturers by total volume.

### Page 2: Deep Dive Analysis
* **Model Performance Matrix:** A detailed matrix showcasing the top 10 individual vehicle models and their percentage share of the overall market.
* **Electric Range Distribution:** A categorization chart splitting the fleet into Low, Medium, and High electric range capabilities.
* **Adoption Trends (BEV vs. PHEV):** An overlapping area chart comparing the historical adoption volume of fully electric versus hybrid vehicles.
* **City Hotspots:** A treemap visualization pinpointing the top cities with the highest concentration of electric vehicles.

## 📁 Files Included
* **`EV-Dashboard.pbix`**: The fully interactive Power BI Desktop project file.
* **`EV-Dashboard.pdf`**: A static, high-resolution PDF export for quick previewing on devices that do not have Power BI software installed.

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Download the `.pbix` file from this repository.
2. Open the file using **Microsoft Power BI Desktop**.
3. Use the built-in **Filters Pane** on the right side of the screen to slice the data by Make, City, or Electric Vehicle Type to uncover specific insights.
